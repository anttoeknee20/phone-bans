Model Equations: Grades 3–8 All Subjects

\[ \text{Louisiana: } \widehat{\text{Proficiency Rate}} = 29.27 + 0.76 \cdot \text{PostBan} \]

\[ \text{South Carolina: } \widehat{\text{Proficiency Rate}} = 39.44 + 3.63 \cdot \text{PostBan} \]

The estimate of the intercept is the predicted score for that row. The other rows estimates show the net difference between that row and the intercept. So, a positive number means it is that much more than the intercept, and a negative means that much less.
